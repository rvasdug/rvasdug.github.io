### Talks
- **I replaced React with a framework I built myself** by _Daniel Rubio_
  - **Abstract:** Want to show a frontend framework I have been working on that uses webcompoents, has zero dependecy and no built step. Build reactive web components with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  - **Speaker Bio:** Software Engineer at Microsoft
- **Processing Subscription Events from Stripe, Apple, and Google Play** by _Caleb Rider_
  - **Abstract:** When a customer purchases or changes a subscription through a provider like Stripe, Apple, or Google Play, that provider can notify your backend asynchronously through webhooks or real-time developer notifications. These events may be duplicated, delayed, or delivered out of order. This talk covers how to authenticate, deduplicate, and normalize subscription events so a customer's product access can be determined.
  - **Speaker Bio:** Software Engineer
- **DevSecOps in 10 minutes** by _Aleksandr Podiachev_
  - **Abstract:** DevSecOps is a fairly new branch of Cybersecurity that combines Software Development, DevOps, and Cybersecurity. It is where Software Developers and DevOps engineers take some of responsibility for patching vulnerabilities and preventing them from reaching the production build. Cybersecurity is treated holistically, rather than something specifically for Cybersecurity teams. We will go over what you can do to practice DevSecOps at your job.
  - **Speaker Bio:** Aleksandr Podiachev is a Software Developer at Booz Allen Hamilton. He is interested in Software Development, Cybersecurity, and Science. His hobbies include video games, thinking of new projects, and looking at what is next technology wise.
- **LLM Pong: Serving a Local LLM Through a Game** by _Abdullah Durrani_
  - **Abstract:** Instead of a chat box, LLM Pong exposes a locally hosted LLM through a game. Visitors play Pong against a bot, player metrics are sent through an API to a local model every 4-5 rallies, and the model's output changes the bot's behavior, creating a live game loop. Work in progress.
  - **Speaker Bio:** Programmer/Developer, self-taught for a couple of years and a coding bootcamp graduate. Currently working in IT infrastructure as an analyst and looking to land a developer role.
- **You've Got (No) Mail: The Email Verification Protocol** by _Kenton Vizdos_
  - **Abstract:** Explore Chrome's new Email Verification Protocol (EVP), how it works behind the scenes, and how it could simplify UX.
  - **Speaker Bio:** Software Engineer
